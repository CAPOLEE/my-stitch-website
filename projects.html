Cultural Sanctuary Ubud, Indonesia

Casa Lumen

Sculpted along the ridge line of the Ayung River valley, Casa Lumen features an expansive hyperbolic timber canopy that channels tropical updrafts for complete passive aeration. Regional reclaimed teak works in concert with low-iron crystalline glazing, blurring interior sanctuary with the jungle canopy.

Residential Retreat Kyoto, Japan

Pavilion Umbra

Positioned beside a tranquil water courtyard at the foot of Mount Hiei, Pavilion Umbra investigates the phenomenological interplay between shadow, stillness, and volcanic stone. Dark basal stone walls anchor floating steel beams and acoustically attenuated floor-to-ceiling glass assemblies.
